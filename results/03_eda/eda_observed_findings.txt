2. KEY EMPIRICAL OBSERVATIONS:
   a) Wind Speed & Gust Dynamics: WSPD exhibits right-skewness (skew=0.46) with mean 5.28 m/s (max 15.30 m/s).
      Wind gust (GST) tracks WSPD with near-perfect linear proportionality (Pearson r = 0.9872).
   b) Barometric Pressure: BAR shows a Gaussian-like distribution centered at mean 1017.58 hPa (std 5.10 hPa),
      ranging from a low of 1000.70 hPa (cyclonic/low-pressure events) to 1033.20 hPa (high-pressure anticyclonic ridges).
   c) Thermal Contrast Between Stations: ATMP shows strong seasonal warming in Gulf Station 42040
      (starting ~15°C in Jan, climbing to >30°C by June). In contrast, Atlantic Station 41002
      was only observed during late spring/summer (mean ATMP 23.36°C, WTMP 25.73°C).
   d) Pressure-Wind Coupling: A mild negative correlation exists between BAR and WSPD (r = -0.116),
      reflecting lower barometric pressure during windier atmospheric disturbances.
   e) Sensor Non-Reporting: WVHT, DPD, and APD have 0 observations across the entire dataset,
      rendering wave height and wave period metrics completely absent.
