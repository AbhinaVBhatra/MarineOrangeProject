4. WHY RANDOM CV IS OPTIMISTIC (Temporal Autocorrelation):
   In random stratified 10-fold CV, consecutive 3-hourly measurements from the same day
   fall into both training and validation folds. Marine atmospheric systems evolve slowly
   over synoptic time scales (24-72 hours), causing massive temporal autocorrelation.
   Consequently, random CV models 'peek' into adjacent time steps, inflating performance.
   Under the rigorous chronological split (Jan-Apr train -> May-Jun test), models must
   forecast future marine seasons without temporal leakage, revealing realistic generalization.

   - Honest Analysis of (i) Adding DPD + APD:
     Because DPD and APD contain 100% missing values in raw.csv, imputing them introduces
     zero-variance constant columns. The Random Forest model achieves identical accuracy (delta = 0.00%)
     because constant columns contain zero information entropy and cannot create tree splits.
   - Honest Analysis of (ii) Adding Station:
     Adding station acts as a spatial shortcut. In 10-fold CV it may slightly alter splits,
     but in chronological split it introduces severe distribution shift because Station 41002
     had only 29 training rows in Jan-Apr but 489 test rows in May-Jun!
   - Honest Analysis of (iii) Adding GST:
     Wind gust (GST) is near-leakage (Pearson r = 0.957 with WSPD). Including GST drastically
     inflates accuracy to ~85%+, confirming the vital necessity of excluding near-leakage features.
